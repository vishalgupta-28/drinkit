About the Distillery

Lagavulin is perhaps the most iconic of all Islay whiskies. Founded in 1816, the distillery is nestled in a small bay guarded by the ruins of Dunyvaig Castle. It is renowned for its slow distillation process and its pear-shaped stills, which contribute to the deep, complex peat smoke and rich, fruity sweetness that has made the 16 Year Old a benchmark for single malt enthusiasts worldwide.
